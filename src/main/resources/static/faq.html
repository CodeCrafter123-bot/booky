Getting started

Is Booky free to use?

Yes — creating a client account and booking appointments is free. Business listing plans may vary; ask your Booky contact for current pricing.

How do I create an account?

Click "Get Started" and register with your name, email, and password. You can register as a client to book appointments, or as a business owner to list your services.

Booking appointments

How do I book an appointment?

Log in, browse businesses from the Browse page, select a business and service, choose an available time slot, and confirm your booking.

Can I cancel or reschedule a booking?

Yes — open "My Bookings" to view, cancel, or manage upcoming appointments. Specific cancellation windows and rescheduling policies are set by each business.

Will I get a confirmation for my booking?

You'll see your booking status update in "My Bookings," and, where email notifications are enabled, you'll also receive a confirmation email.

For business owners

How do I list my business?

Register as a business owner, then add your business profile, services, pricing, and hours from your owner dashboard.

How do I manage incoming bookings?

Your owner dashboard shows all bookings for your business, where you can accept, decline, or review appointment details.

Can I respond to customer reviews?

You can view all reviews left for your business from the owner reviews page.

Account & privacy

How do I update my profile information?

Go to your Profile page after logging in to update your name, email, or phone number.

How is my data protected?

Passwords are stored as secure hashes and authentication uses token-based sessions. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete my account?

Email support@booky.example with your account deletion request, and we'll process it in line with our Privacy Policy.
