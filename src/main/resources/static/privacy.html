Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This Privacy Policy explains how Booky ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website and booking platform (the "Service"). By creating an account or using Booky, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information

  • Full name, email address, and phone number provided at registration
  • Password (stored as a secure hash — we never store plain-text passwords)
  • Account role (client, business owner, or admin)

Business information (for business owners)

  • Business name, description, address, and category
  • Services offered, pricing, and business hours

Booking and usage information

  • Appointment details: service booked, date/time, and status
  • Reviews and ratings you submit
  • Login sessions and authentication tokens

Automatically collected information

  • Basic technical data such as browser type and device information
  • Cookies used for authentication and, where enabled, analytics — see our Cookies section below

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate your sessions
  • To process and display bookings between clients and businesses
  • To send transactional emails (booking confirmations, status updates)
  • To let business owners manage their services, hours, and reviews
  • To maintain platform security and prevent abuse
  • To improve the Service based on aggregated usage patterns

3. How we share information

We do not sell your personal data. Information is shared only in these situations:

  • Between users as needed for bookings — a business you book with can see your name, contact details, and appointment details
  • Service providers — such as email delivery providers, strictly to operate the Service
  • Legal requirements — if required by law, regulation, or valid legal process

4. Data retention

We retain account and booking data for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You can request deletion of your account as described in Section 7.

5. Data security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including password hashing and token-based authentication. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Cookies

Booky uses cookies and similar technologies that are strictly necessary to keep you signed in and to remember your preferences. If analytics or marketing cookies are enabled, they will only be set after you consent via the cookie banner. You can change your preference at any time — see our cookie settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data via your profile settings
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Request a copy of your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@booky.example [REPLACE WITH REAL CONTACT EMAIL].

8. Children's privacy

Booky is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under that age. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will update the "Last updated" date above and, for material changes, provide additional notice.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email us at privacy@booky.example.
